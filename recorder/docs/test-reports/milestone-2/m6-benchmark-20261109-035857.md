# M6 multi-camera performance baseline

2026-11-09 03:58 UTC. 3 round(s) of [10, 15] cameras, 180 s warm-up + 10 min sampling each: about 78 minutes. Cameras beyond N are not recorded during a run. Above 10 cameras the extra ones are copies of real cameras' streams through go2rtc (no extra camera connections).
Sampled every 5 s. Recording disk device: dm-1; camera network: enp1s0.

Above 10 cameras the extra cameras are copies: copy11 of cam01, copy12 of cam02, copy13 of cam03, copy14 of cam04, copy15 of cam05. Each copy takes its camera's main stream and substream from go2rtc's own RTSP restream, so go2rtc, Frigate (recording the main stream, decoding the substream) and the substream archiver do the same work for it as for a real camera. No extra connection goes to the cameras, so the network input shows only the 10 real cameras. After the run the copies' substream folders were deleted (copy11, copy12, copy13, copy14, copy15); Frigate deletes the copies' main-stream files after the retention days.
