# M3: RTSP camera ingestion

## What it does

- Every test camera in `cameras.yaml`, main stream and substream, pulled once by go2rtc.
- Camera passwords only in `/etc/vivo/secrets.env` (root, 600), never in Git.
- Automatic reconnection: go2rtc retries a dropped camera on its own; Frigate's watchdog restarts its
  recorder when the restream comes back.
- Camera table: `docs/camera-table.md`, generated by `tools/probe_cameras.py`
  (model and firmware from `cameras.yaml`; codec, resolution, frame rate, bitrate, keyframe interval measured).

## Test

```bash
sudo tools/probe_cameras.py                      # writes docs/camera-table.md
sudo tests/m3_reconnect.py --cycles 5            # 5 unplug/replug cycles per camera
sudo tests/m3_soak_report.py --hours 24 \
  --planned "2026-10-21T10:00Z/2026-10-21T11:00Z" # after the 24 h run; planned = reconnect test window
```

The reconnect test switches each camera's PoE port through the managed switch
(`POE_BACKEND=command` with `POE_OFF_CMD`/`POE_ON_CMD` in site.env, `poe_port` per camera),
or asks an operator to unplug it (`POE_BACKEND=manual`).

## Not included

ONVIF discovery (M4), installing or cabling cameras on site, certifying camera models beyond the test units.
